    Research prototype, not a product for sale. Mechanics: DDTRobot/diablo_ros2 (Apache-2.0), joint count from Liu et al. 2024; wheels rebuilt to its dimensions, details not checked against the ts-diablo unit. Neuron coordinates: FlyWire annotations. Region colours: FlyWire super_class and cell_class, sampled per region, so density is not to scale. Region functions: summary of the literature. Map and trajectory: project SLAM map. Blocking comparison: closed-loop simulation stage3_009. Decisions: selected segments of real-robot run stage5_005, displacement integrated from commands. Leg and wheel motion and the person are illustrative. Rendering: three.js (MIT). Details in the README.
